📚 Homepage Instructions

How to Use DreamStream for Lucid Dreaming

Play one of the provided playlists while you sleep which will open in your preferred music streaming app. Each playlist includes a subtle reality check tone — the same tone that will repeat throughout your day.

During the day:
	•	If you’re using the DreamStream mobile app, you’ll get a local notification every 45 minutes with the reality check reminder tone. No setup required.
	•	If you’re using the web app, download the tone and set a recurring alarm or reminder manually on your device every 45 minutes.

Why It Works
By consistently associating a specific reality check reminder tone with the action of performing a reality check throughout your waking hours, you are conditioning your mind. This training ensures that when the same tone plays intermittently within the provided sleep-time playlists, your subconscious will prompt you to perform a reality check within your dream — helping you realize that you’re dreaming.

How to Do a Reality Check
A reality check is a quick test to determine if you’re awake or dreaming. Try one of these whenever you hear the tone:
	•	Try pushing your finger through your opposite palm
	•	Pinch your nose shut and try to breathe through it
	•	Look at text or a clock, look away, then look back

What Happens in a Dream
In dreams, reality often breaks down. Your finger might pass through your palm. Text may scramble or change when you look again. You might still breathe through a pinched nose. These impossible results are your cue that you’re dreaming — and that you can take control.

Scientific research indicates the optimal frequency for performing reality checks is approximately 16 times per day at 45-minute intervals.
