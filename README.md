# 🌙 DreamStream
A Lucid Dreaming App

DreamStream is a lucid dreaming companion app that helps you train your brain to perform **reality checks** throughout the day — and within your dreams — using **curated playlists** and a **reality check reminder tone**.

This is a lightweight, privacy-respecting app that works with your existing music streaming services. No accounts, no tracking — just lucid dreaming tools that work.

---

## 🎧 What It Does

- ✅ Plays a **reality check tone** every 45 minutes during the day (mobile app only).
- 🧠 Helps you build the habit of doing reality checks with that specific sound.
- 💤 At night, you stream one of 7 custom playlists — each one includes that same tone, subtly embedded to **trigger dream-based reality checks**.
- 🧘‍♂️ Conditions your subconscious to recognize the tone and respond, even while dreaming.

## 🧠 What Is a Reality Check?

A **reality check** is a quick test to determine if you’re dreaming or awake. These checks help train your brain to question reality — even in dreams.

### Common Reality Check Techniques:
- Try pushing your finger through your palm
- Plug your nose and try to breathe through it
- Look at text or a clock, look away, and look back — does it change?
- Ask yourself: “Am I dreaming right now?”

### How It Works
> Scientific research indicates the **optimal frequency** for reality checks is **16 times per day**, or **once every 45 minutes**.

By consistently associating the **reality check tone** with the act of doing a reality check during waking life, your mind learns to respond automatically.  
Later, when the **same tone** plays from a DreamStream playlist during sleep, your subconscious will prompt you to **do a reality check in your dream**, helping you become aware — and **lucid**.

## 📝 Instructions

### If You’re Using the App (mobile):

### If You’re Using the Web App:

- Use any alarm or reminder app that supports custom sounds.
